Pebble Labs · Experiment 002

Focus Session

A short research session. We quietly watch your blink rate, gaze, and head movement through your webcam while you read or work, then ask how focused you felt a few times along the way.

  • Nothing is recorded or uploaded — your video never leaves this browser. Only numeric stats and your check-in answers are saved.
  • We also log basic interaction signals (like switching tabs) to understand distraction, and at one point during the session you may see a brief on-screen prompt as part of the test.
  • Find a well-lit spot and face your camera directly.
  • Sessions run about 10 minutes. You'll get a couple of quick "still focused?" check-ins along the way.
  • You can stop at any time — nothing bad happens if you do.

Reading passage

The Pebble Labs research program exists to answer one question before any hardware gets built: can passive signals — the way someone blinks, where they look, how still they hold their head — reveal something real about their attention and fatigue? Most wearable products start with a sensor and work backward to a use case. This project starts with the human problem and tests, honestly, whether the signal is there at all. Read this passage naturally, at whatever pace feels normal, and don't worry about performing for the camera — the goal is to see how you actually behave, not how you think you should.
